# Moon surface image

`moon-full.jpg` is NASA's composite of the lunar near side using Clementine
spacecraft imagery from 1994. Credit: NASA / Goddard Space Flight Center.

Source: https://science.nasa.gov/image-detail/amf-gsfc_20171208_archive_e001982/

Original image: https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001982/GSFC_20171208_Archive_e001982~orig.jpg

The original 1536 × 864 image is stored unchanged. The moon card crops the lunar
disc using an SVG viewBox and overlays the illuminated portion with the projected
geometry of a sphere. Waxing phases are lit on the right and waning phases on the
left (north-up view). This is a schematic phase illustration: it does not model
individual crater shadows, libration, or the Moon's rotation relative to the local
horizon. Phase timing uses an approximate mean synodic month.
